AMBER CONFIG

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

Local-first by design. Your SSH connection details, keys, and VPN configuration files are stored only on your device and are never sent to our servers.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Amber Config ("the app," "we," "us"). Amber Config is a VPN and SSH configuration manager available outside the Google Play Store. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to the practices described here.

2. Information we collect

Automatically collected

  • Firebase Analytics — app usage patterns and features accessed, device model and OS version, crash reports, and approximate location (country/region, from IP)
  • AdMob advertising data — ad impressions and interactions, non-personally-identifiable device identifiers, ad performance metrics
  • Google Play Services — basic install and update analytics

Information you provide

  • SSH connection details — hostnames, usernames, ports, and keys you add are stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store these on our servers.
  • VPN configuration data — any config files you create or import stay on your device.

What we don't collect

  • Passwords or SSH private keys (local only)
  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Your actual internet traffic or VPN connection content
  • Financial information

3. How we use this information

  • Analytics & improvement — understanding usage patterns to fix bugs and improve features
  • Advertising — serving ads through Google AdMob (non-personalized by default; you can adjust this in your device settings)
  • Legal compliance — meeting applicable laws and regulations
  • Security — detecting and preventing fraud or abuse

4. Data storage & security

SSH connection details, private keys, and VPN configurations are stored locally using Android's EncryptedSharedPreferences, part of the Android Security library. This data never leaves your device.

Analytics data collected through Firebase and AdMob is encrypted in transit and stored on Google's servers under Google's own security practices. All data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS.

5. Data retention

  • Local data — SSH connections and configurations remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app
  • Analytics data — retained per Firebase's default policies (typically 14–56 months depending on event type)
  • Ad data — follows Google AdMob's own retention policies

6. Third-party services

  • Google Firebase — Firebase Privacy & Security
  • Google AdMob — AdMob Privacy
  • Google Play Services — Google Privacy Policy

7. Your privacy rights

Access & control: view and delete local SSH connections and configurations within the app, opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings, and reset your advertising ID at any time.

Deletion: delete local data directly in the app or by uninstalling it. Analytics data deletion can be requested from Google via your Google Account settings.

GDPR & CCPA: if you're in the EU or California, you have additional rights including access, rectification, erasure, and portability. Contact us for data subject requests.

8. Children's privacy

Amber Config is not intended for users under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we become aware that we have, we'll promptly delete it. For users aged 13–18, parental guidance is recommended before use, since the app handles VPN and SSH configuration.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in our practices, technology, or legal requirements. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page and, where required, an in-app notice.

10. Compliance

  • GDPR — compliant with the EU General Data Protection Regulation
  • CCPA — compliant with the California Consumer Privacy Act
  • COPPA — compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act
  • Google Play policy — adheres to Google Play's User Data Privacy Requirements

Questions about this policy or your data? We respond to privacy inquiries within 30 days.
